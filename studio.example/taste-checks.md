# Taste checks (digest for stage roles)

Ask before showing anything:
1. Own subject, or like the last project?
2. Designed page or placed page?
3. Where does the grid break, and why?
4. One idea, visible in the first screen?
5. Eye path: first, second, third?
6. Is the typography doing the layout?
7. Would a graphic arts professor pass it?
8. Structured and balanced: where is the rest?
9. Could it sit beside the benchmark, finished, for this reader?
10. Can this reader act from here, and reach every page?
