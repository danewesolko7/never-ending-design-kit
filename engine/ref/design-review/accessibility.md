## 6. Accessibility audit (WCAG 2.2 AAA; S6, S7 and S8)
- Contrast measured for every text pair: 7:1, or 4.5:1 for large text. Measure text in SVG and masked type by hand (the lightest pixel).
- Text presentation (1.4.8): flush left, lines under 80 characters, line spacing 1.5.
- 44px targets, visible 3px focus, skip link, landmarks, one H1 and ordered headings, labelled forms with accessible errors, descriptive link text, expanded abbreviations, glossary links, reflow at 320px, reduced motion, forced-colours fallbacks.
- Reading level (3.1.5) and unusual words (3.1.3) stay open until the owner decides; never report AAA compliance while they are open or before keyboard, screen-reader and user testing.
