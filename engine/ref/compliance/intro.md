## Compliance
Every site meets its markets' rules for privacy, consent, accessibility, provider details and security from the first build, not as a late patch. The rules live as data in jurisdictions.json (34 jurisdictions; "all" selects every one); tools/policy.py reads them with brief.json compliance{}. tools/comply.py checks the plan at S2 and the built site at S8 and S9, and writes the handover. The kit makes sure the pages, links and behaviour exist and work. It does not write law: the copywriter drafts the policy pages to the outlines in pages.md, a lawyer approves them, and compliance.legal_review stays "pending" until then. checkpoint.py lists a pending review as a client item.
