## How to search
- **WebSearch** for each query (standard mode first; extended for niche or very recent work). Send several queries in one turn, and vary them by angle (subject, form, period, medium).
- **Open what you cite.** WebFetch the page to confirm what it shows and the technique described. A search snippet is not a reference.
- **See it.** Use image search to look at the visual references, and show the strongest at the milestone. Capture benchmark pages at 1440 and 390 with `tools/shot.js` into `out/bench/` (this project only; never kept in the kit). If a site is blocked, use the browser when connected, or ask for screenshots.
- **Breadth:** no more than two references from one source per sweep, at least two from outside web design (print, maps, packaging, signage, exhibitions, books, the subject's own documents), and current work (the last three years) alongside classics.
- **Rights:** references are for learning, never for copying. Borrow one technique each, never a layout, a brand or an image. Never put a referenced image, or a photo of a public figure, into a deliverable.
