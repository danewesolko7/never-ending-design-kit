## Sweep A: subject (S1)
- **Artefacts (8 to 12):** what the subject makes for itself: documents, tools, packaging, maps, marks, signage, materials, places. Record what each looks like (shapes, marks, colours, type).
- **Peers (4 to 6):** from site-discovery, now looked at as design: what the page leads with, its page form, and what to avoid.
- **Benchmarks (3 to 5):** the professional bar for this content and reader (for example newsroom graphics desks for explainers, top studios' sites for a portfolio). The CD confirms them at G1; capture them into `out/bench/` for this project only.
- Sources to try: the subject's own sites and archives, museum and library collections, trade publications, newsroom graphics (Reuters Graphics, FT, NYT, The Pudding), Our World in Data, government service manuals.
