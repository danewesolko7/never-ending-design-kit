# Inspiration sweep: look outward before designing


Designers fill the wall with references before they draw. Each sweep is a real online search, with real pages and pictures looked at, never a list from memory. First read the technique libraries (`ref/library.md`, then `studio/library.md`) and sweep only for what it lacks. Three sweeps, each before the stage that needs it:

| Sweep | Stage | Looks for | Feeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| A Subject | S1 Research | the subject's own artefacts, peer sites, benchmarks | content-form-map, benchmarks shown at the gates |
| B Structure | S3 Structure, before the concepts | page forms, grids, pacing, editorial structures | the three concepts |
| C Aesthetic | S4 Direction, before any card is drawn | type in use, colour, image treatment, texture, movements | the three direction cards and boards |
