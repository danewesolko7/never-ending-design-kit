## 5. The moment (S4 direction, S6 comp)
A direction names at most one moment per page, two on a long page, each inside its own section, chosen from the subject (library.md section 7: photo chapters, pinned steps, a statement lighting up, a picture developing, an iris to a new ground, rolling figures, giant type with a picture through it). Everything else on the page rests: no fade-in on every section, no parallax on text, no scroll-jacking, nothing that moves only because the page scrolled. Photo- and type-led moments suit places, products and services; shader grounds and scenes only where the subject is itself technical. The moment works without script and under reduced motion as a still.
