## 1. Whether to animate (S4, S7)
Decide by how often the reader meets the element:
- Many times a session (navigation, a menu, filters, a form field): no motion, or a colour change only.
- Now and then (a menu opening, a dialog, a confirmation): a short, plain transition.
- Once (the opening screen, the one moment the direction names): this is where character goes.
Good reasons: show where something came from or went, confirm an action, explain a change, soften a jump. "It looks good" is a reason only for the once-a-visit moment. Never animate what the keyboard triggers repeatedly. Hover motion only where a fine pointer exists (media hover and pointer fine), since a touch tap fires a false hover.
