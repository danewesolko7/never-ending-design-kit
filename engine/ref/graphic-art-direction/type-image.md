## 3. Type as image
- **Photo-filled type** (`background-clip: text` over a darkening gradient), only at display size. Measure the lightest pixel of the fill against the ground in the rendered page (shot.js, then the pixels read in a Playwright canvas); it must meet 4.5:1 for large text. Report the measured figure. Provide a solid fallback in `@supports` and in `forced-colors`.
- **A second voice for evidence** (mono or another face for sources, dates and labels), with one job per voice.
