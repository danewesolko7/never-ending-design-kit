## 0b. Push and pace (the tension budget)
Pushing is half the job; the other half is pacing, so bold work still feels structured.
- **One loud move per screen** (1440 x 900, or 390 x 844 on a phone): a bleed, an oversized, rotated or spaced headline, an overprint, a diagonal, a reversed ground or a broken grid. A second is allowed only if it echoes the first.
- **About 70% order, 30% tension.** Everything that is not the loud move obeys the grid, the shared margins and the spacing scale (design-foundations sections 3 and 4).
- **Rest after noise.** Loud, rest, medium, rest, loud: never five climaxes in a row.
- **Ration, never remove.** Keep the signature move and reduce how often it is loud: the H1 carries the full treatment, section headings a quieter echo (same face, smaller, normal tracking, a weight step). Removing the signature move makes the page plain, not balanced.
- **Repeat the frame, vary the idea.** Margins, label position, heading-to-body gap and stroke weights repeat on every section; the picture, the heading's behaviour and the section's idea vary.
