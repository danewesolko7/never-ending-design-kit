## 4. Image treatment
- **Choose before treating.** Stock photography is fine and often used. Choose shots for subject and viewpoint, avoid the subject's cliches unless the treatment transforms them, and record licences. Diagrams and illustration can be the primary imagery for explainers.
- **One treatment makes the set.** Grow it from the subject's artefacts (texture, marks, cuts, colour by region). A full single-colour overlay is a duotone; check the registry before using one.
- **Layering tools:** blend modes on part of an image (`mix-blend-mode` with `isolation: isolate`), texture from the subject, slices, shape masks (`clip-path`), offset frames.
- **Consider the ground.** If accessibility rules push every ground pale, design at least one dark or reversed ground on purpose.
- **No text on photos.** Labels sit on solid ground beside the image.
