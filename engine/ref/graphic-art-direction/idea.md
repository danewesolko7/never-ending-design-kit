## 0. Start from an idea, not a technique
- Before choosing any technique, write each page's one idea in a sentence, drawn from its content (for example "every number carries its source" or "small parts add up to a whole"). Then choose the technique that shows that idea.
- A technique used on more than about a third of a site's sections has become a template. Vary it or drop it.
- Ask of every section: could this treatment move to another page, or another subject, unchanged? Design so the answer is no.
