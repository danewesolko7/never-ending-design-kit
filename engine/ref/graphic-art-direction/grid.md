## 1. Grid: nest, push the edges, keep the space
After Timothy Samara, *Making and Breaking the Grid*.
- **One page grid, a different nested grid per section** (modular, stepped, masked, data-driven), chosen for what the section says.
- **Push to the edge.** Bleed an element off the left or right page edge (a negative margin of the gutter, with `overflow-x: clip` on the section), and pair it with open space on the other side.
- **Cut by the frame.** What the frame cuts must be empty or decorative, never a label.
- **Space as a shape.** Empty areas are composed, not left over.
- **Sequence as movement.** A fixed step, down or across, turns order into motion.
- **Rules that start mid-grid** can mark where a section's content starts.
- **Never set text in side-by-side columns.** A figure beside one text block is fine.
