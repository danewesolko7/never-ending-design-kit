## 6. Period techniques (when a movement is chosen; see design-movement-library)
Use one only where it says something true about the content:
- **Overprint** for overlap or combination; check text never sits on the darkest overlap.
- **Sequence numerals** for a numbered process (`aria-hidden` when the heading carries the number).
- **Information diagram** (tiers and heavy arrows) for who does what.
- **Proportion by area** (sides by the square root of the value, in percent) for comparing amounts.
- **Proof correction** (`<del>` and `<ins>`) for revised figures.
- **Index typography** (rotated tags, an A to Z bar of 44px links) for lists and glossaries.
- **Leader lines** for labelling the parts of one thing.
- **Calendar fields** (one square per day) for durations.
- **Contrast study and reversal** for accessibility content or emphasis.
