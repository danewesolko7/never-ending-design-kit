## 4. Data storytelling patterns
Sticky graphic, step swap, progress-linked animation, annotated chart, direct labelling, small multiples, word-sized graphics, personalised entry, scale shift with a stated multiplier, unit charts, explorable.

**Proven techniques**: group units into round blocks; scale shift between pages; small multiples over time; solid for actual and outline for projected; a source sentence under each figure's heading; the title states the finding; the title as colour key; highlight one and grey the rest; give and get shown separately; areas drawn to scale (square root of the value).
