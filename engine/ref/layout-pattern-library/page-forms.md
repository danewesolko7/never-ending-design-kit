## 3. Page composition patterns
**Page forms** (choose one per layout; take it from the subject's artefacts where possible): manuscript, atlas (index map then plates), technical document (datasheet), catalogue, timeline, map, specimen sheet, packing line, poster sequence.

**Openers**: thesis statement, cover, answer first, full-bleed object, data-art field, asymmetric split [2-COL].

**Body rhythm**: editorial column rhythm, spanning offsets, inline imagery, pull quote, overlap and layering (sparingly), diagonal or shaped divider, staggered grid, masonry or mosaic, sticky scroll stack, juxtaposition [2-COL].

**Closers**: one call-to-action sentence at scale, or a small list of next links.
