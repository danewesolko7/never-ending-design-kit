## 7. Typography (Butterick, Refactoring UI, Elements of Typographic Style)
The rules are in design-foundations section 5. Choosing and setting notes:
- **Emphasis.** Bold or italic sparingly, never both. Underline only links.
- **Paragraphs.** Space between or indent, never both.
- **OpenType.** Small caps for acronyms; optical sizing where the face has it.
- **Wrapping.** `text-wrap: balance` on headings and `pretty` on paragraphs.
- **Typography as layout.** Hanging lead-ins, stepped stacks, rotated headings, text wrapped round shapes and diagonal planes: see graphic-art-direction section 2.
