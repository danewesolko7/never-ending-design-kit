## Rules for greeked studies
- No colour (greys only), no typefaces, no subject metaphors or props, and no style. Grounds only where grouping needs them.
- True copy length always. Never shorten copy to fit a layout; change the layout, or flag the copy as too long for the copywriter.
- Every section's form must answer: what is the clearest way to explain this to the reader? Draw diagrams as boxes with a note (for example "process, five stages, who does each").
- The whole page, from the header to the footer, including the end of the page.
- Text never sits on an image block.
