## 1. Hierarchy and weight
- Hierarchy comes from scale, weight, contrast and space, in that order.
- About five size levels a page, never more than eight. Neighbouring levels at least 1.25 times apart, or two weight steps apart. Merge sizes that do the same job.
- At least three weights in use. Weight contrast inside a line (heavy subject, light rest) where a heading has a subject and a predicate.
- One entry point per screen, at least twice the visual weight of the next element. A clear second level, then quiet body text.
- Plan it as a map before styling: each level, its size, weight and job.
