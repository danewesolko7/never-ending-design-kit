## 6. Colour
- Set roles first: ground, text, muted text, accent, data, state. Build shades in `oklch()`, distinguished by lightness as well as hue.
- Saturated colour only for actions and key data. Colour carries meaning, never decoration.
- AAA contrast: 7:1 body, 4.5:1 large text, 3:1 graphics. The design must hold in greyscale.
- Design at least one dark or reversed ground on purpose. Never pair red and green (rules/rules.md).
