## 2. Gestalt grouping
| Principle | Rule on the page | Typical failure |
|---|---|---|
| Proximity | A label sits closer to its heading than to anything else; a caption sits closer to its image than to the next block. | Labels floating between sections. |
| Similarity | The same role gets the same style everywhere. | Three label styles for one role. |
| Common region | A ground or frame groups only what belongs together, and its edge means something. | Bands cutting a group in two, or on every section. |
| Continuity | Lines, diagonals and repeated edges lead the eye to the next entry point. | A diagonal that ends nowhere. |
| Closure | A cropped object still reads whole: crop at most about a third of a recognisable shape. | A bleed so large the object becomes noise. |
| Figure and ground | One figure per screen; grounds stay quiet. | Patterned figure on a patterned ground. |
| Focal point | One element breaks the pattern. | Two equal heroes. |
| Uniform connectedness | Join only what is related, by line or shared colour. | Decorative lines linking unrelated sections. |
