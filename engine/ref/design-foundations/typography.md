## 5. Typography
- Body text first: 18 to 21px, a measure of 60 to 75 characters (80 is the AAA limit), line spacing 1.5, flush left, never justified, a good rag.
- Headings: line spacing 0.9 to 1.25, tracking tightened as size grows, lines broken for sense (never split an adjective from its noun or a number from its unit).
- Pairing: one family's weights and axes, or two clearly different families with separate jobs. Avoid overused defaults (Inter, DM Sans, Space Grotesk, Playfair, Bebas).
- Details: true quotes and dashes, non-breaking spaces between number and unit, tabular lining numerals in figures and tables, proportional in text.
- The register must suit the reader and the voice, not only the subject.
