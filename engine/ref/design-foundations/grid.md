## 3. Grid and alignment
- One page grid (manuscript, column, modular, hierarchical), with a baseline rhythm. Sections may nest their own grid inside it (Samara, *Making and Breaking the Grid*).
- At most four strong left edges per desktop screen, shared down the page. No near misses (edges 2 to 12px apart): align them or move them a column apart.
- Never end an element in a gutter. Break the grid deliberately, once per screen, and say why.
- Large type is optically aligned (the stem, not the sidebearing, on the column edge); punctuation hangs.
