## 7. Usability heuristics (Nielsen, applied to pages)
1. **Visibility of status:** the current page in the nav, where the reader is in a sequence, loading and form states.
2. **Match the real world:** the reader's words, not the organisation's; forms the reader recognises.
3. **User control:** clear ways back, skip links, no traps; nothing irreversible without a check.
4. **Consistency and standards:** one name and one style per thing; web conventions kept for links, nav and buttons.
5. **Error prevention:** constraints and clear labels before errors happen.
6. **Recognition over recall:** labels and context on screen; no hidden meanings.
7. **Flexibility:** shortcuts for experienced readers (an index, a glossary, anchors) without burdening new ones.
8. **Aesthetic and minimalist design:** every element earns its place; decoration that carries nothing is cut.
9. **Help with errors:** plain messages that say what happened and how to recover.
10. **Help and documentation:** a glossary, sources and an about page where the subject needs them.
