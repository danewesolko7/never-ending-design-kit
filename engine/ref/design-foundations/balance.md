## 4. Balance and rhythm
- Visual weight comes from size, darkness, saturation, texture, density and isolation.
- Asymmetric balance is the default: a heavy mass on one side needs a counterweight on the other (a small weight far out balances a big one close in). The weight centroid of each screen sits in the middle third, unless it leans on purpose and the next screen leans back.
- Rhythm: loud, rest, medium, rest, loud. After a loud screen comes a quieter one. About 70% order, 30% tension.
- One spacing scale (for example 8, 16, 24, 40, 64, 104, 168). Space between groups is at least twice the space within a group.
- Focal points sit on thirds or grid intersections.
