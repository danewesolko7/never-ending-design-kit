## 3. Postmodernism (1970s to 1980s)
- **Concept:** against the establishment and the grid. "Less is a bore." Celebrate irony, pop culture, decorative excess and imperfection.
- **Feel:** eclectic, rebellious, nostalgic, loud, ironic.
- **Layout:** deliberately break the grid. Overlap elements, scatter small geometric confetti (triangles, squiggles, zigzags), and build collages, frames within frames and dense layers.
- **Texture:** halftone, grainy airbrush gradients, plastic sheen, photocopy, hand-cut paper edges.
- **Type:** radical experiment. Mix unrelated serif, sans and script faces, stretch or skew text, and drop oversized decorative initials in unexpected places. At AAA, keep body text unskewed and readable; experiment in display type only.
- **Tone and copy:** tongue-in-cheek, knowing, conversational, witty, with wordplay and pop references.
- **Touchstones:** Wolfgang Weingart, April Greiman, Paula Scher, Neville Brody, Memphis Group.
- **Prompt keywords:** Memphis Group pattern, April Greiman digital collage, 1980s experimental typography, clashing maximalist layout.
- **Rule:** embrace contradiction. Break alignment, mix high and low culture, and use expressive decoration.
