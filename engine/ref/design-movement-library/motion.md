## 6. Motion graphics and 3D (now)
- **Concept:** the screen is a room with depth, not a flat page. Everything has weight, mass, friction and response.
- **Feel:** futuristic, cinematic, tactile, fluid, cutting-edge.
- **Layout:** deep space with foreground, middle ground and background layers. Layouts change with time, scroll or simulated gravity, and elements morph and interact.
- **Texture:** refraction, metallic chrome, iridescent liquid surfaces and volumetric light with soft shadows. Glassmorphism stays banned under the standing rules; use real depth instead.
- **Type:** kinetic, variable type. Weights morph between thin and bold with interaction or time, and letters can be extruded into 3D. Honour reduced motion with a static final state.
- **Tone and copy:** visionary and sensory, with language about movement, space and technological change.
