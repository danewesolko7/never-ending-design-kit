## 4. Minimalism and flat design (2010s to now)
- **Concept:** radical reduction for screens. Remove visual friction, load fast on every platform, and make scanning immediate.
- **Feel:** premium, very clean, frictionless, accessible, friendly, corporate-tech.
- **Layout:** clean modular blocks and wide, even fields of empty space. Elements are centred or neatly aligned on a flexible web grid, with generous padding and soft containers.
- **Texture:** completely flat, matte colour blocks and no physical texture. Any gradient is smooth, linear or radial, between closely related colours.
- **Type:** geometric or humanist modern sans-serifs with generous line height and spacing. Pick one outside the banned list (not Inter).
- **Tone and copy:** friendly, direct, helpful, scannable. Short sentences, clear lists and bold headings.
- **Touchstones:** Apple interfaces, Google Material Design, Jony Ive, Dieter Rams.
- **Prompt keywords:** flat vector illustration, corporate tech UI, 2D vector graphic, linear gradient background, high-end mobile app interface.
- **Rule:** no depth at all. No skeuomorphic texture, bevels, drop shadows or 3D illusion.
