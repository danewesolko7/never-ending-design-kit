## Precedence (read first)
The owner's standing rules (`rules/rules.md`), the studio registry and WCAG 2.2 AAA always win over a movement's rules. Where they clash, keep the movement's idea and change how it is carried out, then say so in the notes:
- **No justified text blocks** (AAA 1.4.8). A Bauhaus "text brick" becomes a stacked or stepped heading, never a justified paragraph. Body text stays flush left, under 80 characters a line, with line spacing of 1.5.
- **No text clipped by the frame, and no text on photos.** Brutalist overflow and Postmodern collage apply to shapes, rules and images, not to words.
- **Banned defaults stay banned**, even when a movement lists them: Inter, glassmorphism, glow, neon on near-black, gradient text and custom cursors. Choose a period-true alternative from the movement's own touchstones.
- **Motion** always has a reduced-motion state, and is never the only carrier of meaning.
- **Copy:** a movement's tone shapes new copy only when the owner asks for new copy. Approved copy is never rewritten to fit a style.
