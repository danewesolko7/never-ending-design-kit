## 1. Bauhaus and early Modernism (1910s to 1930s)
- **Concept:** design is a machine for communication. Remove the designer's ego, and treat every layout as an architecture or blueprint problem where use decides form.
- **Feel:** industrial, useful, revolutionary, plainly honest, structural.
- **Layout:** thick solid black or red rules carry the structure. Arrange parts on a strict vertical or horizontal axis, or pin them to one bold 45-degree diagonal. Group elements tightly to show mechanical unity.
- **Texture:** raw unbleached paper, newsprint, matte lithographic ink. No gradients and no gloss.
- **Type:** text as a physical brick. Use heavy geometric or early sans-serif faces, often in capitals. Stack words vertically or build them into dense rectangles. At AAA, do this with stacked headings, not justified body text.
- **Tone and copy:** like a manifesto. Urgent, stark, declarative, no filler. Short absolute statements ("The machine is our medium."). Banned words: organic, elegant, luxurious, whimsical.
- **Touchstones:** Laszlo Moholy-Nagy, Herbert Bayer, Joost Schmidt, Walter Gropius.
- **Prompt keywords:** De Stijl architecture, lithographic block printing, constructivist poster, red and black geometric rules, raw unbleached paper texture.
- **Rule:** every element must do a structural job. If it is only decoration, delete it.
