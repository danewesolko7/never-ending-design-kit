## Replacement rule
The page, and each section on it, takes the form its content already has in the real world: an estimate, a parts diagram, a floor plan, a timetable, a label, a catalogue. If a banned pattern comes to mind, name the content's real-world form and use that instead.

Use only forms the customer actually receives, holds or walks through. Why: an internal job board once looked distinctive but exposed tracking and other clients' data.
