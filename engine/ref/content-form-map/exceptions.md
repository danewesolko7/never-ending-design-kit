## Sanctioned exceptions (each needs a written reason in the table)
- Conventional pattern: allowed when the audience expects it and it speeds a task (a sign-in, a checkout, standard navigation, a process they must follow). One per page. Government and regulated sites follow legal and platform conventions.
- Proof: named case studies, credentials, a reference list, or reviews linked to their source. Real and attributed only.
- Headlines: display headlines are 2 to 4 words; section headings and page titles may be descriptive, plain words, about 8 words at most.
