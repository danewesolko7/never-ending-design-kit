## Steps
1. Page skeleton first: name the real-world form of the whole page and why. Blur test: if all content were blurred, would the layout still read as a stock landing page? If yes, choose another form.
2. For each page, list the sections the reader's jobs require.
3. Produce a table: section | the content | its real-world form | why that form | grid break used, if any.
4. Mark where the call to action sits, what feeds it (for example, a self-check whose answers fill the request), and confirm it is reachable early on a phone.
5. Write the real display headline and first line for each section from discovery facts, plus a descriptive heading where one is needed.
