# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and anywhere someone represents
the project.

In short: be kind and specific, assume good faith, critique work rather than people, and make room for people who
are new. Harassment, insults and publishing someone's private information are not tolerated.

## Reporting
Report a concern privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting (the repository's **Security** tab,
**Report a vulnerability**), starting the title with "Conduct". Reports are read only by the maintainers, handled in
confidence, and answered within 5 working days. Maintainers who are party to a report step aside from handling it.

Enforcement follows the Covenant's guidelines: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
